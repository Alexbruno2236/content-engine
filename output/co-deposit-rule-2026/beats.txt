A professional move-out clean averages 360 dollars in 2026.
Most tenants pay it out of fear.
In Colorado, as of January first,
that fear is mostly obsolete.
House Bill 25-1249 rewrote
what counts as normal wear and tear.
General uncleanliness is now included.
Your landlord cannot deduct for it.
The exception is narrow:
only if the place is substantially less clean
than the day you moved in.
Automatic cleaning fee clauses are now void.
But the deadline matters more than the cleaning.
The landlord has thirty days
to return your deposit.
Miss it, and they waive every right
to keep any of it.
The statement must be itemized,
with photographs, receipts, and invoices.
Ask in writing,
and they have fourteen days to produce it.
Withhold more than 125 percent of the real damage
and it is presumed unreasonable.
Bad faith means treble damages.
Photograph everything the day you move in.
Especially the oven.
