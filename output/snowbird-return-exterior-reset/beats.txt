One hundred dollars a day.
That is what a Florida HOA
can fine you for algae on your roof
or mildew on your stucco.
It caps at a thousand
for one continuing violation.
About a million people winter in Florida
and summer somewhere else.
Their houses sit empty from June through September.
That is the rainy season,
and the fastest growing season of the year
for anything living on an exterior surface.
Nobody is there to watch it spread.
But the association cannot just send a bill.
Under Florida Statute 720.305,
you get fourteen days written notice
and a hearing.
The fine needs a committee of three,
none of them officers or directors.
A fine imposed without that hearing
is unenforceable.
The cheapest version of this
is the one that never starts.
Clean it in September,
before the flight back.
Come home to a house.
Not a letter.
